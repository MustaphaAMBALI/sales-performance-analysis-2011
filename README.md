##  Project Objective

To analyze 2011 sales and profitability trends, uncover high-performing regions and product categories, and present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard that communicates insights effectively to stakeholders.

## Data Source

The dataset used for this analysis is the publicly available [Sample - Superstore Sales (Excel).xls](https://community.tableau.com/s/question/0D54T00000CWeX8SAL/sample-superstore-sales-excelxls) provided by Tableau.  
It contains sales records from **2009 to 2012**, covering orders, products, customers, regions, and profits across different categories.  
For this project, the data was filtered to focus specifically on **2011** sales.

## Key Dashboard Element

Key features of the dashboard include:

- **Sales Trend Over Time** – Line chart showing sales progression throughout the year  
- **Profit Margin Trend Over Time** – Line chart tracking profitability performance  
- **Sales by Category** – Bar chart highlighting category-level sales performance  
- **Profit by Category** – Donut chart as complementary view for category profitability  
- **Sales by Region** – Bar chart comparing sales across regions  
- **KPI Cards** – Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Top Sales Region, with YoY % difference integrated under KPIs  

---

##  Methodology

1. Cleaned and filtered dataset to **2011** records  
2. Built PivotTables to aggregate key metrics  
3. Created calculated fields:  
   - **Profit Margin** = Profit / Sales  
   - **Year-over-Year % Difference** for KPIs  
4. Designed interactive visuals with slicers for region and category  
5. Applied conditional formatting for positive/negative indicator colors  
6. Focused on limiting chart quantity to emphasize clarity and insights  

---

##  Insights and Recommendations

-  **Central region** led sales performance in 2011  
-  **Technology category** achieved highest sales and profitability  
-  Overall **profit margin** at 11.68% suggests improvement potential  
-  **East region** showed strong sales but lower relative profit
